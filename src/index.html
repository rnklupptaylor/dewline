Kelvin equilibrium
RH = exp[−4γ(T)Vm / (RTD)]

γ(T) = 0.1215 − 0.0001662 T N/m; Vm = 18 × 10⁻⁶ m³/mol; R = 8.3145 J/(mol K). T is in kelvin and D in metres.

Hemispherical meniscus and matching hemispherical pore bottom, rm ≈ D/2. The bowl joins the cylindrical neck tangentially, removing the sharp bottom corner. Liquid water is assumed below 0 °C. Freezing, nucleation and hysteresis are outside this model. Optical contrast and the fill/empty animation are illustrative; the transition timing is not a prediction of condensation kinetics.

Preset (b) uses “filled by 90% RH” across the full temperature range. At −10 °C, an 18.23 nm pore fills at 86.90% RH.
